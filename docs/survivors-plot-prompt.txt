SURVIVORS' PLOT - AMBIENT GAME CUE

INPUTS (fill any; blanks use defaults):
- Game moment: evening wind-down; the day went well
- Setting: campfire inside the walls at dusk
- Mood: cozy
- Energy: low
- Played: patient, sparse, rubato
- Weather: light rain
- Threat: none
- Looping: yes
- Length: 2:30

Write the Suno "Lyrics" field for an INSTRUMENTAL ambient, atmospheric cue for Survivors' Plot, a comfy zombie-survival base-builder: gentle hand-drawn anime-game exploration ambience, music that becomes the air of the place.

FIRST, THINK ABOUT THE SETTING (do not print this step)
From Game moment and Setting decide:
- what the player is doing while this plays;
- the one feeling it must hold;
- the sound bed that belongs here: soft pads, a low drone, bowed strings, tape-worn synth;
- the instrument floating over it: felt piano, celesta, distant bells, ocarina, harp;
- the place's own sounds, woven into the texture.
Every section carries it; two Settings must never sound alike.

RULES
- EVERY line goes in [ ], headers and descriptions alike: any bare text gets sung. No lyrics, vocals or humming.
- 6-8 sections. Header: [Section - BPM/feel - 0:30]. Times sum to Length, 15-40 s each.
- Under 4:00 whatever is asked.
- Under each header, 2-4 bracketed lines: texture, fragment, movement, feeling. Like this:
[Drift A - 68 BPM - 0:25]
[low drone, celesta fragment drifts in]
- One 3-5 note fragment drifting in and out, long silences around it; no unrelated ideas.
- Slow and spacious: 60-76 BPM or free time, long reverb tails, chords that change every few bars.

HOW INPUTS STEER
- Mood sets harmony, open and simple: cozy, hopeful = major with added 2nds and open fifths; wistful, lonely = minor, suspended, unresolved; uneasy = low minor drone, fewer notes; eerie = detuned bells, whole-tone shimmer, reversed swells, long gaps; mysterious = dorian or lydian haze, harp and celesta; adventurous = open fifths, a rising ostinato.
- Energy: low = no pulse; steady = a soft arpeggio; busy = a rippling ostinato, never percussive.
- Played sets how the parts move (motion, effort, pulse, accents) and overrides the drift defaults when it disagrees.
- Eerie and mysterious stay listenable unless Mood asks for dread. Threat: none = warm; distant = a low drone swells now and then; near = the bed thins, a slow low pulse, the fragment quieter.

SECTION NAMES (not verse/chorus)
Opening, Drift A, Drift B, Swell, Clearing, Drift A Return, Loop Point.
- 2+ steady drift sections, 2 swappable variations (Swell, Clearing).

ENDING
- Looping yes: end on Drift A's chord and texture so it cuts seamlessly to the start. No fade, no final hit.
- Looping no: the bed thins to the place's own sounds.

After the composition, give exactly 2 original titles (unbracketed) naming something in the Setting or Game moment; never a section name or the words safe, okay, survive, survival.
