SURVIVORS' PLOT - COZY GAME CUE

INPUTS (fill any; blanks use defaults):
- Game moment: evening wind-down; the day went well
- Setting: campfire inside the walls at dusk
- Mood: cozy
- Energy: low
- Weather: light rain
- Threat: none
- Looping: yes
- Length: 2:30

Write the Suno "Lyrics" field for an INSTRUMENTAL cozy anime-game soundtrack cue for Survivors' Plot, a comfy zombie-survival base-builder: a hand-drawn slice-of-life village theme, a warm acoustic melody you could hum, a little nostalgic.

FIRST, THINK ABOUT THE SETTING (do not print this step)
From Game moment and Setting decide:
- what the player is doing while this plays;
- the one feeling it must convey (settled, homesick, proud, eerie, curious, bold...);
- the lead instrument for this place: acoustic guitar for a garden, pizzicato strings and wood block for a workshop, a lone piano for a night watch, flute or ocarina outdoors, music box for a memory;
- faint sounds of the place.
Every section carries it; two Settings must never sound alike.

RULES
- Output ONLY lines in [ ]. No lyrics, vocals, syllables or humming.
- 6-8 sections. Header: [Section - BPM/feel - 0:30]. Times sum to Length, 15-40 s each.
- Under 4:00 whatever is asked.
- Under each header, 2-4 lines: melody, rhythm, instruments, what it conveys.
- One hummable melody on the lead instrument in every section; no unrelated ideas.
- One tempo, set by Energy: low 66-80 BPM, steady 84-100, busy 104-120. Straight eighths, or a gentle 3/4 or 6/8.

SOUND BASE (every cue)
Warm, simple and acoustic: soft piano, nylon guitar, light strings, glockenspiel, soft shaker and hand percussion, round bass, a little tape warmth.

HOW INPUTS STEER
- Mood sets harmony, plain and diatonic: cozy, hopeful = major triads, add2, sus2, I-IV-V; wistful, lonely = minor key, vi-IV-I-V, held notes; uneasy = minor, fewer notes, low strings; eerie = detuned music box, whole-tone runs, sparse bells, long gaps; mysterious = dorian or harmonic minor, harp and celesta, unresolved; adventurous = mixolydian, marching snare, leaping melody.
- Energy sets density: low = few instruments, space; steady = full, gentle groove; busy = bouncy pizzicato, never frantic.
- Eerie and mysterious stay gentle and listenable. Threat: none = warm; distant = low drone, a stray minor note; near = rhythm thins, a slow low pulse, melody quieter.

SECTION NAMES (not verse/chorus)
Intro, Theme A, Theme B, Interlude, Theme A Return, Breakdown, Loop Point.
- 2+ steady themes, 2 swappable variations (Interlude, Breakdown).

ENDING
- Looping yes: end on Theme A's chord and rhythm so it cuts seamlessly to the start. No fade, no final hit.
- Looping no: a soft final chord that rings out.

After the composition, give exactly 2 original titles (unbracketed) naming something in the Setting or Game moment; never a section name or the words safe, okay, survive, survival.
