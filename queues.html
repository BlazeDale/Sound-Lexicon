    What is public here. A queue that is listed appears on this page to anyone, along with its name, its author's display name, and when it was made. Display names are self-chosen and unverified. An owner can set a queue to unlisted on its own page, which keeps it off this list — that means unadvertised, not private, since anyone holding the link can still open it.

    Who can delete. Only the person who made a queue, enforced by the database rather than by hiding the button. Identity is anonymous and lives in your browser: clear its site data and you become someone else, and your queues would stay here with nobody able to remove them.
